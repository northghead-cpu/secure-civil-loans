Who We Are

Riverbanc Technology Ltd is a Zambian financial technology company operating a membership-based digital marketplace that connects salaried employees with participating commercial banks and licensed microfinance institutions.

Through the Riverbanc platform, members can compare available financial products, submit digital loan applications, explore refinancing opportunities and make more informed financial decisions. Riverbanc aims to make product information easier to compare while supporting financial institutions with efficient digital engagement.

Important distinction: Riverbanc is a marketplace, not a lender. It does not provide loans, accept deposits or make lending decisions. Product terms, eligibility assessments, approvals and lending decisions remain the responsibility of participating financial institutions.

Partnership Opportunities

Riverbanc seeks to build practical, long-term partnerships with employers, public institutions, commercial banks and licensed microfinance institutions. Employer engagement may include introducing employees to the marketplace, coordinating staff communications and exploring appropriate payroll processes subject to organisational approval, applicable requirements and employee authorisation.

  • Digital visibility for financial products offered by participating institutions.
  • Structured digital engagement with prospective borrowers.
  • Employee access to a consolidated comparison experience.
  • Governance, information security and implementation responsibilities defined through the agreed partnership framework.
